Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply quickly.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the car's size and condition. An exterior and interior detail usually takes most of a day. Paint correction and ceramic coating can take one to three days, because the coating needs time to cure.

What's the difference between wax and a ceramic coating?

Wax sits on top of the paint and wears off within weeks. A ceramic coating bonds to the paint, lasts far longer, and is harder, glossier and more resistant to heat, dust and water spots.

Can paint correction remove every scratch?

Swirls, holograms and light scratches, yes. If a scratch has gone through the clear coat (you can catch it with a fingernail) it needs paint repair. We'll inspect it and tell you honestly before we start.

How do I look after a ceramic-coated car?

Wash it gently with a pH-neutral shampoo, avoid automatic brush car washes, and dry it with a clean microfibre towel. We'll give you a simple aftercare guide when you collect your car.
