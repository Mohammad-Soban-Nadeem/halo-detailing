The Halo Process

Four stages between dull and dazzling.

Nothing rushed, nothing skipped. This is what happens to your car in our hands.

01

Foam & decontaminate

A thick snow-foam pre-wash lifts grit before anything touches the paint. Then we hand wash, dissolve iron fallout and tar, and clay the surface until it's glass-smooth.

02

Correct the paint

Machine polishing removes swirl marks, holograms and light scratches one panel at a time, checked under an inspection light as we go.

03

Coat & protect

A ceramic coating bonds to the corrected paint, locking in the gloss so water, dust and dirt slide straight off.

04

Inspect & hand over

Every panel is inspected under studio lights before we hand back your keys. If it isn't perfect, it isn't finished.
